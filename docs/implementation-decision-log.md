## D-009 — Accelerated evidence-gated automation plan

D-009 supersedes only the conflicting timing, strategy-research, validation, and canary language identified here. D-001 through D-008 otherwise remain governing. Approval records future implementation requirements; it does not authorize immediate DXtrade work or change the current Stage A deployment.

### Strategy research and backtest design

- Use 12 months of completed, UTC-aligned Binance `BTCUSDT` data as the primary research dataset. Preserve `source` and `symbol`; Binance remains prohibited from placing or controlling Tradeify trades.
- Divide the year chronologically into eight development months, two validation months, and two untouched holdout months. Perform rolling walk-forward checks within the first ten months. Freeze a candidate before it sees the holdout.
- Test four controlled slots: a Donchian-channel breakout/trend strategy; a moving-average or time-series-momentum/trend-pullback strategy; the Bollinger/RSI mean-reversion hypothesis; and one explainable data-discovered candidate.
- A discovered candidate must be formalized from development data before validation and holdout. Do not mine unlimited indicator combinations and select a historical coincidence.
- Model next-bar execution without lookahead, verified DXtrade fees and lot rules, realistic slippage, the minimum hold, both account floors, all daily controls, the one-position limit, and server-side-stop requirements.
- Run two reports: the governed simulation enforcing the live profit lock and all downside controls, and an uncapped-upside shadow analysis that measures opportunity after the profit lock without authorizing additional live trades.
- Collect at least 1,000 shuffled trade-sequence simulations at the initial $100 risk. A live candidate must produce zero simulated account failures in those runs.

### Qualification and regime-direction routing

- The combined routed system must be net profitable after verified costs over the full year, the validation period, and the untouched holdout period; have positive expectancy; avoid dependence on one exceptional trade; remain stable around reasonable nearby parameters; and produce zero simulated account failures, daily-loss-limit breaches, or active-floor violations.
- Each strategy-direction-regime route requires at least 30 historical trades before it may control live orders. A profitable route with fewer observations remains shadow-only.
- Evaluate long and short performance separately. A strategy that is profitable long but unprofitable short may trade only long; its short side is discarded. A strategy that is profitable short but unprofitable long may trade only short; its long side is discarded.
- Use a deterministic, pretested market-regime decision tree. At most one qualified strategy may control a trade at a time. Other candidates continue in shadow mode.
- Rank passing routes primarily by governed net profit after costs, then profit relative to drawdown, stability, and holdout behavior. Use uncapped shadow results only to identify future upside proposals.
- Permit only the approved winner for the detected regime and direction. Uncertain, extreme, stale, malformed, or conflicting conditions produce no trade. Never switch strategies while a position is open.
- Backtest the complete routing tree as one combined system. Preserve the one-open-position maximum across all strategies and instruments.

### Accelerated pre-launch validation

- Remove the separate requirement for 14 consecutive pre-launch days of bot-floor/dashboard matching.
- Before restricted automatic trading, require at least 24 continuous hours of Binance-versus-DXtrade comparison and one observed 22:00 UTC Tradeify account-day reset.
- Verify that balance, equity, daily floor, MLL floor, and active floor match the activated DXtrade dashboard immediately before and after the reset.
- Verify completed UTC boundaries and measurable pass/fail thresholds for missing, duplicate, malformed, delayed, stale, misaligned, and materially divergent bars.
- Run the comparison and reset observation concurrently with the remaining build so they add no post-build delay when already complete.
- Continue Binance/DXtrade comparison for at least seven days during the canary. A comparison failure pauses automation, but clean continuing comparison does not block an otherwise qualified Day-3 promotion from $100 to $200 risk.

### Restricted canary and 12-day hard cap

- Begin approved automatic execution at a maximum of $100 risk per trade only after the full build, D-004 security, account discovery, D-009 backtest/data requirements, execution/reconciliation tests, and an explicit activation approval pass.
- Review the canary on Tradeify account Days 3, 6, 9, and 12.
- On Days 3, 6, or 9, promote to the configured $200 Stage 1 cap when cumulative live P&L is positive, at least three trades have closed, and every signal, entry, fill, fee, server-side stop, exit, database record, and reconciliation check is correct.
- Before Day 12, positive results with fewer than three trades remain at $100 until the next review or the third completed trade.
- On Day 12, the canary must end in promotion or removal. A positive, operationally clean strategy may promote to $200 with the three-trade minimum waived only when at least one complete live trade verified the entire execution lifecycle.
- At Day 12, a zero- or negative-P&L live strategy is removed rather than promoted; the next qualified shadow candidate may be evaluated under a separately tracked canary. No-trade results end that strategy's canary for review and do not justify a risk increase.
- Any stop rejection, duplicate order, reconciliation mismatch, unexplained position, safety halt, daily hard stop, or other critical execution defect pauses automation and prohibits promotion until corrected and rechecked.
- The configured $300 Stage 2 cap still requires the approved $53,000 balance threshold and any applicable later review.

### Persistent evidence and controlled optimization

- Persist source-tagged bars; regime inputs and classifications; every live and shadow signal; selected and rejected strategy reasons; full risk-gate snapshots; requested and actual entries; unique client order codes; fills, fees, slippage, stops, targets, exits, maximum favorable/adverse excursion, dollar and R-multiple results, holding time, daily account values, lock events, configuration version, and Git commit.
- Produce daily summaries by strategy, direction, and regime, including governed results and uncapped-upside shadow opportunity.
- Live evidence may generate an optimization proposal, but the bot must not silently rewrite or activate its own rules. Every material optimization requires an exact proposal, backtest against untouched data, comparison with the deployed version, owner approval, decision-log update, tests, and verified deployment.
- All existing account safeguards remain binding unless a later approved decision explicitly changes them: daily loss controls, profit lock, active-floor margin, one open position, durable pause, safety halt, shared risk gate, idempotency, reconciliation, confirmed server-side stops, audit logging, and both execution locks.

## D-010 — Activated-account rule reconciliation

### Confirmed account facts

- Tradeify Crypto Instant Funding account; active DXtrade access.
- Starting balance: $50,000.
- Profit Split 95% add-on.
- Maximum daily loss: $1,500.
- Maximum drawdown amount: $3,000.

- Tradeify's published DXtrade Platform Guide expressly allows API automation by a bot owned by the trader and confirms Market, Limit, Stop, and Stop-Limit orders plus pre-entry SL/TP support.

### Conservative working rules for unresolved mechanics

- Model the maximum-loss floor as End-of-Trade trailing: it ratchets after each newly profitable closed-trade high, never moves down, and caps at $50,000.
- Use 22:00 UTC as the Tradeify account-day reset.
- Enforce no more than 2:1 leverage on all pairs.
- Model 0.04% commission on entry and 0.04% on exit.
- Backtests may include the contract's 0.033% overnight cost as a conservative buffer until read-only account evidence confirms whether it is charged.
- Apply the 20% consistency requirement and first-payout drawdown lock.
- Use live equity, including unrealized P&L, for breach protection.
- Treat the platform/account metrics as the system of record. Any mismatch pauses progression and requires reconciliation.
- Binance remains data-only and may never place, route, or control Tradeify orders.
